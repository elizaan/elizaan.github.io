Ishrat Jahan Eliza.

Human-centered AI & data visualization

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at the SCI Institute, University of Utah, advised by Prof. Valerio Pascucci.

I build and study tools that help people work with complex data: from AI agents for scientific exploration to accessible chart descriptions and visual interfaces for clinicians. My work brings together system building and studies with scientists, clinicians, and blind and low-vision readers.

Previously, I studied Computer Science and Engineering at BUET and worked on the data science team at IQVIA.
